Maharashtra Agricultural Scheme

Launched: 2018 by the Government of Maharashtra

Objective: To provide financial assistance for high-value crops and improve farmers’ income and agricultural productivity.

Key Features

  • Provides subsidy/financial support for crops like horticulture, vegetables, fruits, and cash crops
  • Encourages modern agricultural practices and technology adoption
  • Supports small and marginal farmers
  • Promotes value addition and marketing of crops
  • Funds are transferred directly to farmers’ bank accounts (DBT)

Benefits

  • Increases farmer income
  • Improves crop quality and productivity
  • Reduces dependency on middlemen
  • Encourages sustainable and diversified farming

Eligibility

Farmers of Maharashtra, with priority to small and marginal farmers

Implementing Authority

Government of Maharashtra
