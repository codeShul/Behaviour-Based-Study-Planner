Seed Distribution Scheme

Scheme: Seed Distribution Scheme (under National Food Security Mission / State Agriculture Schemes)

Objective: To provide quality certified seeds to farmers at subsidized rates in order to increase crop productivity and ensure food security.

Key Features

  • Distribution of high-yielding variety (HYV) and certified seeds
  • Covers crops such as paddy, wheat, pulses, oilseeds, and millets
  • Seeds are provided at subsidized prices
  • Encourages use of improved and disease-resistant seed varieties
  • Implemented through State Agriculture Departments, Krushi Seva Kendras, and cooperatives

Benefits

  • Improves crop yield and quality
  • Reduces risk of crop failure
  • Lowers input cost for farmers
  • Promotes adoption of modern agricultural practices

Eligibility

All farmers, including small and marginal farmers

Implementing Authority

Central and State Governments
