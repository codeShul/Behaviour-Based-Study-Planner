Farm Mechanization Scheme

(Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization – SMAM)

Launched: 2014 by the Government of India

Objective: To promote use of modern agricultural machinery and reduce dependency on manual labor, especially for small and marginal farmers.

Key Features

  • Provides subsidy and financial assistance for purchase of agricultural machinery and equipment
  • Supports custom hiring centers (CHCs) so small farmers can rent machines
  • Promotes machinery like tractors, power tillers, seed drills, harvesters, and sprayers
  • Encourages adoption of energy-efficient and advanced farm equipment
  • Focus on small, marginal, women, and SC/ST farmers

Benefits

  • Reduces labor cost and time
  • Increases farm efficiency and productivity
  • Ensures timely agricultural operations
  • Improves farmers’ income

Eligibility

All farmers, with priority to small and marginal farmers

Implementing Authority

Central and State Governments
