Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Yojana

Launched: 2023 by the Government of Maharashtra

Objective: To provide financial assistance and ensure income support to farmers in Maharashtra, supplementing the central PM-Kisan scheme.

Key Features

  • Eligible farmers receive ₹6,000 per year under the scheme
  • Amount is given in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each
  • Benefits are provided in addition to PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi
  • Money is transferred directly to farmers’ bank accounts (DBT)
  • Helps farmers meet agricultural and household expenses

Benefits

  • Provides financial stability to farmers
  • Supports small and marginal farmers
  • Reduces dependency on loans
  • Encourages continued engagement in agriculture

Eligibility

  • Farmers who are beneficiaries of PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi
  • Must be residents of Maharashtra

Implementing Authority

Government of Maharashtra
