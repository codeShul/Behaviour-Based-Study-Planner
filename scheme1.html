Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Launched: 2016 by the Government of India

Objective: To provide crop insurance coverage and financial support to farmers against crop loss or damage due to natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

Key Features

  • Covers food crops, oilseeds, and commercial/horticultural crops
  • Protection against drought, flood, cyclone, hailstorm, pest attack, and diseases
  • Low premium rates for farmers:
    • 2% of sum insured for Kharif crops
    • 1.5% for Rabi crops
    • 5% for commercial & horticultural crops
  • Remaining premium is paid by Central and State Governments
  • Claims are settled directly into the farmer’s bank account (DBT)
  • Uses technology like satellites, drones, and mobile apps for quick assessment

Benefits

  • Ensures income stability for farmers
  • Encourages adoption of modern farming practices
  • Reduces dependency on loans after crop loss

Eligibility

All farmers including loanee and non-loanee farmers

Official Portal: pmfby.gov.in
