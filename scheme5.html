Farm Pond Scheme

Scheme: Farm Pond Scheme (under Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana / State Agriculture Schemes)

Objective: To harvest and store rainwater on farms for protective irrigation and improve water availability during dry periods.

Key Features

  • Construction of farm ponds to collect rainwater
  • Provides subsidy and financial assistance to farmers
  • Stored water used for protective irrigation, livestock, and fisheries
  • Helps recharge groundwater
  • Especially useful in rainfed and drought-prone areas

Benefits

  • Ensures water availability during dry spells
  • Increases crop productivity
  • Reduces dependency on monsoon rains
  • Supports multiple cropping and allied activities

Eligibility

All farmers including small and marginal farmers

Implementing Authority

Central and State Governments
