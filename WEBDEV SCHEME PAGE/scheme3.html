Drip Irrigation Scheme

Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

Launched: 2015 by the Government of India

Objective: To promote water-efficient irrigation through drip and sprinkler systems and ensure “More Crop Per Drop”.

Key Features

  • Provides financial assistance and subsidy for drip irrigation systems
  • Supplies water directly to the root zone of plants
  • Reduces water wastage, evaporation, and runoff
  • Suitable for horticulture, vegetables, sugarcane, cotton, and field crops
  • Encourages adoption of modern irrigation technology

Benefits

  • Saves 30–50% water
  • Increases crop yield and quality
  • Reduces fertilizer and electricity consumption
  • Lowers labor and operational costs

Eligibility

All farmers including small and marginal farmers

Official Portal: pmksy.gov.in
