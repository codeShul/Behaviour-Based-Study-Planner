Soil Health Card Scheme

Launched: 2015 by the Government of India

Objective: To assess the nutrient status of soil and provide farmers with recommendations to improve soil health and crop productivity.

Key Features

  • Soil samples are collected and tested every 2–3 years
  • Soil Health Card provides information on macro-nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), secondary nutrients, and micro-nutrients
  • Recommends appropriate fertilizer dosage and soil amendments
  • Helps in balanced use of fertilizers
  • Cards are issued free of cost to farmers

Benefits

  • Improves soil fertility and crop yield
  • Reduces input cost by avoiding excess fertilizer use
  • Promotes sustainable farming practices
  • Increases farmer awareness about soil management

Eligibility

All farmers across India

Official Portal: soilhealth.dac.gov.in
