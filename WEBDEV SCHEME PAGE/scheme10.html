Baliraja Shetkari Sanman Yojna

Launched: By the Government of Maharashtra (exact year depends on official notification)

Objective: To recognize and financially support exemplary farmers (Balirajas) who contribute to agricultural development, adopt innovative farming practices, and improve productivity.

Key Features

  • Rewards farmers for outstanding performance in agriculture
  • Encourages adoption of modern and sustainable farming techniques
  • Promotes crop diversification and quality enhancement
  • Enhances income of small and marginal farmers

Benefits

  • Financial assistance or incentives for selected farmers
  • Public recognition to encourage community participation
  • Motivation to adopt efficient and innovative agricultural practices

Eligibility

  • Farmers of Maharashtra
  • Preference often given to small and marginal farmers demonstrating exemplary agricultural practices

Implementing Authority

  • Government of Maharashtra
  • Local agricultural offices facilitate nominations and verification
