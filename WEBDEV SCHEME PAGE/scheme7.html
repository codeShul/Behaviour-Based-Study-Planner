Jalyukt Shivar Scheme

Launched: 2014 by the Government of Maharashtra

Objective: To make Maharashtra drought-free by improving water conservation and ensuring water availability for agriculture and drinking purposes.

Key Features

  • Deepening and widening of streams, rivers, and nullahs
  • Construction of check dams, farm ponds, and percolation tanks
  • Desilting of existing water bodies
  • Recharge of groundwater
  • Focus on water-scarce and drought-prone villages

Benefits

  • Increases groundwater levels
  • Improves irrigation capacity
  • Reduces dependency on monsoon rainfall
  • Enhances agricultural productivity
  • Ensures drinking water availability

Eligibility / Coverage

Implemented at village level across Maharashtra

Implementing Authority

Government of Maharashtra
